Word frequencies for NeuroVault's autocorrect
=============================================

File: frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt (82,765 English words with how often each is used)
From: SymSpell by Wolf Garbe, https://github.com/wolfgarbe/SymSpell
Licence of the repository: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Wolf Garbe

Where the counts come from (SymSpell's README): "two data sources were combined by intersection":
- Google Books Ngram data, http://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv2.html
  Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- SCOWL, Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists, http://wordlist.aspell.net/ (licence: http://wordlist.aspell.net/scowl-readme/)

NeuroVault reads the file to rank spelling suggestions on this PC; nothing is sent anywhere.
